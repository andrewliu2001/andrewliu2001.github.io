Bio

I am a machine learning researcher at Flagship Pioneering focusing on efficient models for drug discovery. I am currently focused on factorized and linear I/O modules for long‑sequence biomolecular modeling. Previously, I received my MS in Computer Science and BS in Applied Mathematics (Magna Cum Laude) at Columbia University. I was also an intern at Alibaba Group, where I was part of the Qwen team working on RLHF.
